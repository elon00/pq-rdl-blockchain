# QMoosa / RDL Token Economics

Version 1.0 — Draft

## Supply Policy

The project specifies an **unlimited maximum token supply**. This is a ceiling policy of `no fixed maximum`, not a permission for arbitrary or hidden minting.

## Issuance Requirements

Any future issuance mechanism must be explicit in protocol code and documentation and must define:

- issuance authority
- issuance schedule or algorithm
- validator/operator reward formula
- governance authority for parameter changes
- auditability of issued supply
- accounting and monitoring of inflation

Until those protocol rules are implemented and verified, no exact future supply, inflation rate, or reward amount is considered final.

## Node Operator Rewards

### Testnet

Testnet rewards may be experimental, temporary, capped by governance parameters, or unavailable. They must not be marketed as guaranteed income or a promise of future Mainnet value.

### Mainnet

Mainnet rewards, if enabled, must be defined by protocol rules and governance, transparently recorded, and reviewed for applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

## Governance

Changes to monetary policy should be versioned, publicly documented, and authorized through the project's final governance model. DAO participation, voting thresholds, and execution controls are subject to the governance specification when finalized.

## Accounting Principle

Every mint or reward allocation should be independently reconstructable from protocol records. The project must not use fabricated balances, validator counts, APR/APY figures, or supply metrics.

## Status

This document defines the current policy direction. It does not claim that unlimited issuance or final Mainnet rewards are already implemented or deployed.
