# PQ-RDL Compliance, Global Standards and Reality Rulebook
## Developer Baseline

## Mandatory Engineering Principles
1. Do not fabricate network, audit, TPS, validator, PQC, compliance, or decentralization claims.
2. Separate prototype, devnet, testnet and mainnet status clearly.
3. Keep reproducible builds, dependency controls, security testing and incident records.
4. Protect secrets and follow least-privilege access.
5. Obtain independent security review before production claims.
6. Apply jurisdiction-specific legal review before token sales, public rewards, DAO treasury operation, or mainnet launch.
7. Respect applicable privacy, data-protection, consumer-protection, sanctions, tax and financial-regulation requirements.
8. Maintain versioned public documentation and changelogs.
9. Treat Supreme Court decisions and Indian law as binding only where legally applicable; do not claim a special “Supreme Court blockchain approval” unless a specific verified authority supports it.
10. This rulebook is an engineering baseline, not legal advice or a legal compliance certificate.

## Reality Standard
A feature is VERIFIED only when reproducible evidence supports the exact claim. Otherwise label it prototype, simulation, local test, pending, or NOT_VERIFIED.
