# RDL Constitution and Reality Charter

## Article I — Purpose
The Republic of Divine Light (RDL) is a research and prototype ecosystem exploring post-quantum cryptography, Conway cellular automata, AI-assisted systems, blockchain concepts, and Web 4.0 governance.

## Article II — Truth and Representation
1. Simulations must be labelled as simulations.
2. In-memory state must not be represented as a public blockchain.
3. Random identifiers, mock signatures, and UI values are not cryptographic or on-chain proof.
4. Performance, TPS, security, audit, compliance, or deployment claims require supporting evidence.

## Article III — Evidence Standard
A claim may be marked **VERIFIED** only when reproducible evidence exists, such as passing tests, provider job identifiers, transaction explorer records, deployed bytecode, audit reports, or independent review.

Otherwise the status must be **PROTOTYPE**, **SIMULATION**, **RESEARCH**, or **UNVERIFIED**.

## Article IV — Security
1. Secrets and private keys must not be committed.
2. Server-side credentials must not be exposed to browser clients.
3. Demonstration identifiers must not be labelled as real cryptographic keys or signatures.
4. Security-sensitive changes require tests and review.

## Article V — AI Accountability
AI may assist with analysis, planning, and generation. AI output is not automatically correct, legally binding, cryptographically verified, or sovereign authority.

## Article VI — Canonical Source of Truth
1. `pq-rdl-blockchain` is the canonical runnable implementation repository.
2. `Republic-of-Divine-Light` is the identity and governance landing repository.
3. New runnable protocol code belongs in the canonical implementation unless the architecture is formally changed.
4. Duplicate implementations must not create conflicting claims of canonical status.

## Article VII — Release Law
A release may be described as production-ready only after its documented tests pass and applicable deployment, security, cryptographic, and operational evidence exists.

## Article VIII — Amendment
This charter may evolve through documented repository changes, provided amendments preserve the Truth and Evidence Standard.

**Canonical implementation:** `elon00/pq-rdl-blockchain`
